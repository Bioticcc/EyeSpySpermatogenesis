How to review

Does the yellow outline trace one tubule?

The yellow line is the program's outline of the candidate. Judge the outline, not the neighbours visible in the corners. Click a crop (or use ← →), then press a number. The next crop is selected automatically. Press 0 to clear. Dashed borders are candidates the algorithm already flagged; review them too, so the flags themselves can be checked. For each section, also estimate how many tubules got no crop at all (open the overview overlay and look for tubules without a number). This is not the "Not a tubule" verdict. Verdicts are saved in this browser until you export.
