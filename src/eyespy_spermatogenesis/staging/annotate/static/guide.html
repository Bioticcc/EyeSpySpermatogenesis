Guide for annotators

How to stage tubules

Each picture shows one seminiferous tubule from a PAS-stained mouse testis section. Press the key for the stage you see (I–XII). Each label is saved as soon as you press the key. That's all.

  1. Sign in
  2. See how far we are
  3. Choose what to stage
  4. Stage a tubule
  5. Save and finish
  6. Optional: compare with the models
1

Sign in

Click Open the annotation page. Type your name and choose Staging, then Start.

  • Spell your name the same way every time. Your labels are filed under it. Names used before appear as buttons under the box, so you can click yours.
  • There is no password on this screen. You already proved who you are with the code we emailed you.
  • Just want to look around? Click Just look around (guest) instead. No name is needed, and nothing you label is saved: when you log out you can choose to save your labels under a name, and if you close the tab they are lost. To save as you go, sign in with your name.
2

See how far we are

After you sign in, the Progress page opens. You can reach it at any time from the left-hand menu.

  • Top boxes: totals for everyone. How many tubules have a stage, how many have been staged by two or more people, and how many you have done.
  • By section: one row per tissue section. Have a stage counts tubules staged by anyone, 2+ people counts those staged by at least two people, and You counts your own.
  • Labels per stage shows how the labels so far divide up, one colour per person, and Labels per person how many each person has given. These counts are the only place names appear: the page never shows who gave which stage.
3

Choose what to stage

There are two ways to choose. Use whichever you prefer.

A. From a list

Click Annotate in the menu. At the top, choose a Section (or All sections) and what to Show:

  • Not staged by me (the usual choice): tubules you haven't staged yet, starting with ones nobody has staged.
  • No stage from anyone yet: only tubules that nobody has staged.
  • All accepted tubules: every tubule, including ones you have already staged.

B. From a map of the section

On Progress, click a row under By section. The whole section opens. Tubules that already have a stage are coloured and show their numeral. Uncoloured tubules have no stage yet. Click any tubule to open it for staging. Untick Overlay (or press O) to see the section on its own, without outlines or stages.

4

Stage a tubule

Look at the tubule and press the key for its stage. The label is saved and the next tubule appears. The yellow line marks the tubule's outline.

You can also click the stage buttons on the right instead of pressing keys.

  • Zoom: scroll the mouse wheel. Move around: drag. Reset: double-click. O shows or hides the outline.
  • Skip: → or Space. Go back: ←. Remove your label: Delete.
  • Other people's stages appear on the right, under yours, without their names. If you disagree, simply give your own stage. Disagreement between experts is useful information, not a mistake.
  • If the picture is cut off, blurred, or shows more than one tubule, press B.
  • Some sections were staged on paper earlier. For those, a Paper stage panel shows the handwritten mark. You don't have to do anything with it. If you notice the computer misread the handwriting, press F and then the stage written on the paper.
  • Keys & help in the menu lists every key.
5

Save and finish

You never need to press save. Each label is stored on the server as soon as you press the key. The bottom of the left-hand menu confirms it:

  • ✓ All saved means everything you've done is stored.
  • ⚠ … not saved yet, retrying… means the connection dropped. Keep the page open; it keeps retrying on its own.

When you finish, click Log out in the menu. You can stop at any point and pick up where you left off later: Not staged by me only shows tubules you haven't done.

6

Optional: compare with the models

Sign in with Model review instead of Staging to give your stage for a fixed set of tubules and then see what the paper stage and two computer models said. Blind mode hides their answers until you have given yours. The Overview page then shows whole sections staged by each model. Click a tubule there to see every tubule the model gave the same stage.

Model review answers are kept separate from your staging labels.
